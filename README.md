
# 🧠 ClarityCare Suicide Analytics Dashboard – Power BI Case Study

Welcome to my portfolio, where I showcase my projects, achievements, and passion for data!

---

## 📄 Project Summary

This project is a Power BI case study developed for **ClarityCare**, a mental health advocacy organization focused on reducing suicide rates through data-driven insights. The dashboard leverages **AI visuals** and **intermediate DAX functions** to uncover the demographic, economic, and geographic patterns influencing global suicide statistics.

Using a dataset of over **6.7 million recorded suicides**, the analysis reveals key risk factors including **gender, age group, generation, and GDP per capita**, empowering ClarityCare to strategically direct its mental health interventions.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Boomers** account for the highest number of suicides globally (33.89% of total), with rates significantly higher than other generations.
- **Males** are far more likely to die by suicide, with numbers exceeding females in every age category.
- **Middle-aged adults (35–54 years)** are the most affected demographic group.
- Suicide rates show clear **correlations with GDP per capita**, varying significantly across countries and economic brackets.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To empower ClarityCare with a powerful decision-support tool that provides clarity on:
- Who is most at risk?
- Where should resources be focused?
- How can interventions be tailored demographically and regionally?
